together we play. so that together we can grow.

ABBF is a movement that breathes life into the dreams of persons with disabilities through the liberating power of adventure and outdoor sports. From marathons and tandem cycling to scuba diving, paragliding and mountaineering.

so here's what we do. and how we do it.

  • Empowering every individual

    Our heart beats for every person, regardless of disability or background. We craft experiences where everyone finds a safe space to enjoy and thrive, so every interaction between PwDs and able-bodied people becomes a journey of understanding and empathy.

  • Sport as a catalyst for change

    We're proud pioneers of accessible adventure sports in India, having touched nearly 15,000 lives. Milestones include the Manali to Khardung La tandem cycling expedition, India's first open water SCUBA diving and paramotoring events for PwDs, and inclusive climbs to Kilimanjaro.

  • Bridging communities with expertise

    We work across many kinds of disability and with a global outreach, orchestrating adventure activities tailored for PwDs with a focus on safety, inclusivity and empowerment.

  • Adventure in every step

    Inclusive treks around Pune are conversations about inclusion that combat the sedentary lives many PwDs are pushed into. Cycling expeditions like M2K and the Tour of Nilgiris celebrate unity and play. SCUBA diving and paragliding offer a new perspective on life.

our verticals.

A multifaceted approach to true inclusion.

  • for the individual

    Celebrating every story of achievement, treating each participant as family, and sensitively catering to the needs of PwDs.

  • for the corporate

    Shattering misconceptions and fostering acceptance through outdoor sports events, from treks to marathons.

  • research

    Using adventure activities as a tool for inclusion, through data analysis, academic research and impact studies.

  • skill building

    Building the confidence and articulation of PwDs through interactive activities, and showcasing their talents to the corporate world.
