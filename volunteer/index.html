why people volunteer with us.

  • A life-changing journey

    Imagine guiding a visually impaired person along a trekking trail, or helping someone feel the thrill of paragliding for the first time. These experiences shape your perspective for good.

  • Skills you can't learn at a desk

    You'll learn to communicate with a diverse group, solve problems on the go and lead, skills that matter in your career and your life.

  • Connections that last

    You'll meet other volunteers, persons with disabilities and corporate leaders who care about the same things you do. Many of these become lifelong friendships.

  • Part of a larger movement

    Watch a child take their first steps with a new prosthetic, or the joy on someone's face as they cross the finish line at one of India's biggest marathons.
