our vision and mission

Four ideas hold ABBF together. Pick one to read it in full.

to build an inclusive world for persons with and without disability.

That is ABBF's sole reason to be. Everything we run is aimed at eradicating the stigma that still surrounds disability today.

a world where no one is left behind.

“A world where people with and without disabilities come together as equals and truly belong.”

Divyanshu Ganatra, founder, in the 2025-26 Annual Impact Report (opens in a new tab)

to promote empathy and inclusivity for all, through outdoor sport.

By organising outdoor sports activities, we encourage dialogue and build empathy between persons with and without disability. Treks, tandem cycling, marathons, SCUBA diving and paragliding put everyone on the same trail, as equals.

For every participant, with or without disability, these interactions result in:

  • enhanced life skills
  • increased self-confidence
  • better social skills
  • a heightened awareness of the need for an equitable world

the beginning of a long, rewarding journey.

At nineteen, Divyanshu Ganatra lost his eyesight to glaucoma. Despite the challenges, he pursued a career as a clinical psychologist and became India's first blind pilot.

Why? Because he simply could not reconcile with what the world was trying to tell him: that the rest of his life would be spent making cane furniture and chalk pieces. That need to do more, be more, say more led to the birth of Adventures Beyond Barriers Foundation. An organisation aimed at eradicating the stigma that surrounds disability even today.

In effect, Divyanshu lost his sight, but gained a vision: to promote empathy and inclusivity for all; to create a world where no one is left behind. And that is the very heart of ABBF.
