how ABBF supports you.

  • Adventure on your terms

    Your disability doesn't define you, your spirit of adventure does. Paragliding, scuba diving or mountain trails: our activities are designed so you can take part safely and confidently.

  • Run alongside corporate leaders

    Take your place at the start line of the TATA Mumbai Marathon, TCS World 10K, Vedanta Delhi Half Marathon or TATA Steel Kolkata, with corporate leaders running alongside you.

  • Education without limits

    We help you access scholarship programmes so your education doesn't come to a halt, and support your academic and professional growth.

  • Access to devices that help

    Through the Help A Child Walk campaign, ABBF has helped hundreds of children access orthotic and prosthetic devices and take their first steps.

  • Changing perceptions

    Our events give persons with disability a platform to show their strength and potential, shifting public attitudes along the way.

  • A community that gets it

    Join a community of people who understand your journey, with resources, support and opportunities to connect.
