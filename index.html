Inspirational quote

why we exist. even though we shouldn't have to.

Stigma, judgement, invisibility. Persons with disabilities have faced these for centuries. And yet, in this most modern of ages, their exclusion from mainstream life still comes as news to many.

Fortunately, this story can be rewritten. Especially if more allies like you join us in our journey to bring lasting change in the form of disability inclusion. In the form of a global community that is at once emboldened, energetic, and empathetic.

And what better way to encourage this sense of community than sport! Sport brings people together, no matter their age, race, religion, or physical ability. It creates bonds between persons with and without disabilities. It is not a one-and-done event. It is an experience that stays with you forever.

real change. by real people. for real people.

From climbing mountains to running marathons. From rappelling to scuba diving. From tandem cycling to paragliding. We've organised thousands of inclusive sporting experiences, as varied as the people taking part.

In 2024 alone, ABBF and our allies raised funds to help underprivileged children across the country walk for the first time in their lives.

We've brought thousands of persons with disabilities and able-bodied persons together to change attitudes and break barriers. We've helped corporate organisations generate thousands of volunteer hours through inclusive CSR activities that build a stronger inclusive culture. These efforts have brought us awards from many distinguished organisations. But the greatest reward continues to be the empowerment of people who have been marginalised for too long.
